About Salama Den

As a private family-owned business, Salama Den Solutions Limited (SDS) was founded in 2010 but has since expanded to accommodate more shareholders and converted to a limited liability company. From humble beginnings at Pioneer House Nairobi, Kenya, SDS has now grown into national fully-integrated electrical contractor of choice. For over 13 years of experience and employing the best skill set and resources, we aim to provide the best integrated electrical and renewable energy services and quality second to none at cost-effective Infrastructure Solutions to our clients as we provide value for money.
SDS has continued to grow its client base and expand, managing larger and more complex jobs, while retaining its core values and service. Over this time the company has also expanded from Nairobi County to all over the country and is looking forward to make international leap as it positions itself in new market segments. We have our vision set to deepen our market foot-print within the country, in East Africa countries as we plan to operate and expand into other promising markets within Africa.
We are proud of our values, ethics and culture which are at the heart of our company and set the standards for how we operate. Our business is driven by the needs of our clients, our partners and our people and all the stakeholders in general.
